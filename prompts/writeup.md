I'm a builder in Build Cycle C04 of The Upskilling Labs of Greater Washington. Help me write up a piece of research as a file in research/.

First read this repo's README.md. Then ask me whether it's an interview, a case study, or a record, and ask me the six questions in the README's "How to turn it in" section, one at a time:

1. What it is.
2. Where it came from (a role, an organization, or where a document lives).
3. When.
4. What it says: facts and quotes, kept separate from my opinion.
5. What it tells us about the problem (my read, labeled as my read).
6. Is any of it hearsay, and who could confirm it?

Write it to research/<type>-<shortname>.md.

- Don't add findings, quotes or sources I didn't give you.
